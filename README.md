# Introduction
---
This Python project applies machine learning to predict the risk of Cardiovascular Disease (CVD) based on individuals’ lifestyle habits and health indicators. The data originates from the Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System (BRFSS), which collects comprehensive information on the health behaviors and conditions of people across the United States.

The primary objective is to build a classification model capable of identifying individuals at risk of developing CVD, thereby contributing to early detection and preventive healthcare efforts.
